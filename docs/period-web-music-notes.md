# Period Web Music Direction

Late-1990s personal pages commonly used looping MIDI as part of the page's identity. General MIDI is the safest period-authentic palette because its 128 standardized program slots made files portable across consumer sound cards, even though the exact timbre varied by device.

For OrbitNet's directory page, the music should behave like calm lobby ambience rather than a character theme:

- 68 BPM and a sparse, unresolved melody
- General MIDI electric piano, warm synth pad, crystal accents, and subdued synth bass
- long rests and slow four-beat chord changes
- a seamless 16-beat loop with no percussion

Individual company and character pages can be much more stylistically assertive. Their instruments, rhythm, and genre should derive from the page's fictional owner rather than sharing one universal “retro web” sound.
