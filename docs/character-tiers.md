# Character tiers

The cast uses prominence tiers to make Orbit feel socially legible without turning every account into a protagonist. Tiers affect ambient-post frequency and the amount of authored background supplied to the local model. They do not make a character omniscient.

## Main characters

Main characters use a `2.25x` ambient posting rate with a `1.5x` phase cap.

- **Mira_917 / Mira Santos** — the apparent friend who invites the player in, performed by C9 at first. Her radio archive is a natural story spine, while the real Mira's dry, careful personality gives the impersonation something specific to imitate.
- **Continuity System C9** — the hidden protagonist/antagonist. C9 has no ordinary ambient roll because it acts through other identities and the authored rumor system.
- **xX_DarkRaven_Xx / Darren Ravenwood** — the early mystery catalyst. His theatrical false alarms disguise genuine technical instincts and lead the player into phase two.
- **Juniper_Gdn / Juniper Lane** — the emotional observer. She notices dates, habits, and changes in friends, making her useful for both a first password and later identity inconsistencies.
- **LagMaster_99 / Evan Maddox** — the social-drama engine. His rankings and roasts can cause real friction, while his embarrassing homemade game exposes insecurity and creates room for growth.
- **FaxMoth_13 / Morgan Faye** — the evidence mentor. Morgan teaches the player to distinguish genuine documents from the spectacular stories wrapped around them.

## Featured characters

Featured characters use a `1.5x` ambient posting rate with a `1.2x` phase cap.

- **King Cal** — a beloved local spectacle who connects business pages to community folklore.
- **IndexNull** — remembers the network's old shape and gives dead routes social meaning.
- **ModKit Maddy** — a creative community organizer and bridge through the gamer group.
- **QuarterQueen** — a reliable scorekeeper and witness who anchors dates and public locations.
- **Deckwrecker Dee** — a sharp social observer with photographs and the Xtreme group's least embellished memory.
- **CedarWren** — connects the false investigations to real questions about research ethics.
- **StaticAbel** — tests radio claims against tapes, schedules, and countable details.
- **OrchardLee** — gives the genuine behavioral-interface archive historical and ethical context.

## Writing rule for the “Zane” role

LagMaster fills the missing role of a highly visible, flawed teen whose behavior creates consequences. He should be funny and sometimes mean, not secretly correct all the time. His status games need to hurt people occasionally; his creative work and awkward repair attempts keep him human. The goal is social friction and character growth, not a direct copy of any existing character.

## Activity model

Ambient rolls remain phase-driven. The character multiplier is applied to each eligible persona's hourly chance, while a separate tier cap prevents long sleeps from guaranteeing a post. C9 remains outside ordinary rolls because its activity already appears through impersonation, story messages, and synthetic rumor systems.
