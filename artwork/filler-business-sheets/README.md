# Local-business filler sheets

These two retained 5x5 ImageGen source sheets supply the ten lightweight
search-only business homepages. The 50 individual in-game crops are in
`assets/images/filler-business/` at a maximum of 180x180 pixels.

Generation mode: built-in ImageGen.

## Company-art sheet

Final prompt summary:

> Create exactly 25 distinct low-resolution late-1990s local-business logo,
> mascot, sale-burst, membership-card, and company-art cells in a 5x5 grid
> separated by thick black gutters. Use the homemade visual vocabulary of
> MS Paint, bundled clip art, early Photoshop, Corel-style effects, cheap
> desktop publishing, primitive bevels, crude gradients, and small-business
> web graphics. Cover ten fictional businesses: a lighthouse-themed VHS rental
> shop, sock-themed laundromat, florist, travel agency, moth-themed copy shop,
> safari furniture outlet, family dentist, plumbing company, neighborhood
> credit union, and hair salon. Include 25 specifically assigned symbols and
> supporting graphics. No real brands, readable company names, watermarks, or
> polished modern identity design.

## Product-and-service photo sheet

Final prompt summary:

> Create exactly 25 distinct low-resolution late-1990s local-business photos
> in a 5x5 grid separated by thick black gutters. Use cheap 1996-1999 35mm,
> newspaper-ad, VHS-frame, and early consumer-digital aesthetics with faded
> color, grain, direct flash, fluorescent lighting, crooked framing, scan dust,
> and JPEG softness. Show storefronts, interiors, staff, products, and services
> for the same ten fictional businesses: VHS rentals, washers and folded
> laundry, flower arrangements and delivery, a travel office, copy and fax
> machines, a crowded furniture showroom, dental office, plumbing van and
> repair, credit-union branch and teller, and hair salon. No real brands,
> modern objects, legible logos, or watermarks.
