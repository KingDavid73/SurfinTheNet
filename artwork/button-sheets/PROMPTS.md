# Amateur directory-button sheets

These are the active source sheets for the 180px directory graphics in
`assets/images/navigation-buttons/`.

All three prompts shared this production direction:

> A square 4x4 contact sheet of sixteen separate small, low-resolution,
> creator-made 1996–2001 web graphics. Ordinary users made them with MS Paint,
> early Paint Shop Pro, scanner software, clip-art CDs, and beginner Photoshop
> tutorials. Preserve 256-color dithering, crude lasso edges, jagged shadows,
> preset bevels, stretched clip art, scanner dust, JPEG halos, awkward
> composition, and visibly different skill levels. No modern UI, polished
> professional retro art, readable lettering, or watermark. Keep each graphic
> inside its cell and readable after downsampling to 180px.

## `amateur-zone-gamer-4x4.png`

Cell order: eight OrbitNet zones; LagMaster, VelvetMage, PlayerFour, ModKit
Maddy, QuarterQueen, CodeDex; Juniper; Mira. Each cell uses the character or
zone's established materials, ranging from cheap chrome game graphics and
glitter clip art to scanned stationery, graph paper, and cassette/radio panels.

## `amateur-xtreme-pet-4x4.png`

Cell order: Dee, Cole, Nico, Ty, Troy, Ollie, Viktor, DarkRaven; Carla, Ray,
Bea, Hal, Iris, Sam; MossMunch and Blipzo fandom buttons. Media includes
photocopied zines, dirt-ramp doodles, rave-plastic bevels, scanned surf photos,
sticker bombing, amateur luxury templates, pet-photo stationery, and fan art.

## `amateur-fandom-oldweb-business-4x4.png`

Cell order: StarThimble, PRISM//5, GEMWELL, Atlas of Orra; RoadHog Ron, Dot's
old page, Dot's new page, Colonel Hal, Railroad Lenny, BigBass Bob; ByteBarn,
Cosmic Crust, Paws & Claws, PULSE/NET, VANTA2, and CUBIT. The sheet mixes tape
archive collage, fantasy-map scans, family-page templates, hobby photography,
small-business clip art, and amateur attempts at corporate console graphics.

The earlier 2x4 sheets remain beside these files as rejected higher-polish
explorations. They are not used by the game.
